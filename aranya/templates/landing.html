Questions

Straight answers

Is this the official Karnataka Forest Department site?

No. Aranya is independent and unofficial, with no affiliation to or endorsement from the Forest Department. The official portal is aranyavihaara.karnataka.gov.in and that is where all trek bookings and payments happen.

Can I book a trek through Aranya?

No, and that's deliberate. Aranya only shows you what the portal is already showing publicly, gathered into one view. You book on the official portal. The ₹{{ price }} is for access to the board, and buys nothing on the portal itself.

Will it alert me when a slot opens?

No. Bookings open at a fixed time that everyone already knows, and this portal doesn't cancel or re-release tickets — so there's no surprise moment worth alerting on. Aranya is a board you check, not a race you win. Anyone selling you alerts for this is selling you something that doesn't help.

How current are the numbers?

The board re-checks continuously and shows when it last updated. It reads the portal's own public pages, so if the portal is slow, wrong or down, the board reflects that. Always confirm on the official portal before promising a seat to a client.

What happens after {{ access_days }} days?

Access simply stops. There's no auto-renew and no subscription to cancel — nothing is charged again unless you choose to pay again. Your trek list is kept, so it's all still there when you come back.

How do I pay?

Create an account and you'll see the payment instructions. Online card payment is being set up; right now access is enabled manually as soon as payment reaches us, usually within a few hours.

Can I share my login with my team?

An account is for one person. There's a limit on how many devices can watch the board at once, and heavy sharing may get an account suspended. If you need several people on it, get in touch and we'll work something out.
